Cairn Notes for Creators — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Cairn Notes for Creators ("Cairn") is a note-taking app for talks, meetings and ideas. It is built so that your notes belong to you.

What we collect

Nothing. Cairn has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer runs no server that receives your data.

Where your data lives

Your notes, recordings, photos, people and AI drafts are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. They are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on. Deleting the app deletes them.

AI features (optional)

AI features only work after you connect an AI provider of your choice (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or your own server) with your own key. When you use an AI feature, the notes, transcripts or photos needed for that request are sent directly from your iPhone to that provider. They don't pass through the developer. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to that data. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Transcription

On-device transcription uses Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone. If you choose online transcription, the recording is sent directly to the provider you set up, as described above.

Sharing and exports

Cairn shares something only when you do it yourself, for example exporting an event, sharing a post or saving a photo to your library.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record talks, meetings and voice notes.
  • Camera / photo library: to take or attach photos, and to save photos you choose.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations differ by country and state. Ask for consent where it is required.

Children

Cairn is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
